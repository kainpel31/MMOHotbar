SkyUI - keycap art
==================

Interface/STB_Keycaps.swf in this archive is not our artwork.

It is SkyUI's interface/skyui/buttonart.swf (character 153, exported as
"ButtonArt") with a second "STBKeycap" export added so the plugin can reach it,
and the number-row key faces flattened to bare digits.

Credit belongs to:

  SkyUI Team                     interface/skyui/buttonart.swf

SkyUI's permissions allow using and modifying its assets as long as SkyUI is
credited, and forbid using them in paid mods. Its "seek permission from
Psychosteve and Jelidity" note applies to the icons_*.swf files, not to
buttonart.swf. Do not sell this asset or ship it in a paid mod.

The plugin itself (MMOHotbar.dll) is GPL-3.0 and is a separate matter -- see
LICENSE in the main archive. This SWF is not covered by that licence.
