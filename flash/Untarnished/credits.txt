STB Hotkey System - keycaps
===========================

Interface/STB_Keycaps.swf in this archive is not my artwork.

It is built from SkyUI's buttonart.swf. Credit belongs to the SkyUI Team.

SkyUI's permissions allow using and modifying its assets as long as SkyUI is
credited. Do not sell this asset or ship it in a paid mod. (The "seek permission
from Psychosteve and Jelidity" note in SkyUI's terms applies to the icons_*.swf
files, not to buttonart.swf.)

A second set, built from Untarnished UI's art for anyone running that interface,
is a separate download on the mod page.

The plugin itself (STB_HotkeySystem.dll) is GPL-3.0 -- see LICENSE. That licence
covers the code, not this SWF.

Rebuilding the file from your own copy of SkyUI, and adapting the keycaps to any
other interface, is documented in flash/README.md at:

  https://github.com/STB-Team/STB-Hotkey-System
